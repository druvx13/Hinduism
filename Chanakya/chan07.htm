Chanakya Niti

Chapter 7

                               Adhyāya 7

07-01

> अर्थनाशं मनस्तापं गृहे दुश्चरितानि च ।
> वञ्चनं चापमानं च मतिमान्न प्रकाशयेत् ॥ ०७-०१ ॥

A wise man should not reveal the loss of wealth, mental torment, misdeeds at
home, being cheated, or being dishonoured.


07-02

> धनधान्यप्रयोगेषु विद्यासङ्ग्रहणे तथा ।
> आहारे व्यवहारे च त्यक्तलज्जः सुखी भवेत् ॥ ०७-०२ ॥

In the management of grain and wealth, in the acquisition of learning, in
eating, and in worldly dealings, one should abandon all sense of shame and
thus be happy.


07-03

> सन्तोषामृततृप्तानां यत्सुखं शान्तिरेव च ।
> न च तद्धनलुब्धानामितश्चेतश्च धावताम् ॥ ०७-०३ ॥

The happiness that belongs to those who are fully satisfied with the nectar of
contentment and who are at peace — that happiness is not experienced by those
who are greedy for wealth and run hither and thither.


07-04

> सन्तोषस्त्रिषु कर्तव्यः स्वदारे भोजने धने ।
> त्रिषु चैव न कर्तव्योऽध्ययने जपदानयोः ॥ ०७-०४ ॥

Contentment should be practiced in three things: in one's own wife, in food,
and in wealth. However, contentment must never be practiced in these three: in
study, in prayer, and in charity.


07-05

> विप्रयोर्विप्रवह्न्योश्च दम्पत्योः स्वामिभृत्ययोः ।
> अन्तरेण न गन्तव्यं हलस्य वृषभस्य च ॥ ०७-०५ ॥

One should not come between two Brahmins, a Brahmin and his sacrificial fire,
husband and wife, master and servant, or a plough and its ox.


07-06

> पादाभ्यां न स्पृशेदग्निं गुरुं ब्राह्मणमेव च ।
> नैव गां न कुमारीं च न वृद्धं न शिशुं तथा ॥ ०७-०६ ॥

One should not touch fire, a preceptor, or a Brahmin with one's feet; nor
should one touch a cow, a maiden, an old person, or a child.


07-07

> शकटं पञ्चहस्तेन दशहस्तेन वाजिनम् ।
> गजं हस्तसहस्रेण देशत्यागेन दुर्जनम् ॥ ०७-०७ ॥

A cart should be kept at a distance of five hastas; a horse at ten hastas; an
elephant at a thousand hastas; and a wicked man by abandoning the very
country.


07-08

> हस्ती अङ्कुशमात्रेण वाजी हस्तेन ताड्यते ।
> शृङ्गी लगुडहस्तेन खड्गहस्तेन दुर्जनः ॥ ०७-०८ ॥

An elephant is controlled by a mere goad; a horse is struck by a hand; horned
animals are controlled with a stick; but a wicked man must be controlled with
a sword in hand.


07-09

> तुष्यन्ति भोजने विप्रा मयूरा घनगर्जिते ।
> साधवः परसम्पत्तौ खलाः परविपत्तिषु ॥ ०७-०९ ॥

Brahmins are pleased by a good meal; peacocks by the roar of clouds; the
virtuous by the prosperity of others; but the wicked only by the misfortunes
of others.


07-10

> अनुलोमेन बलिनं प्रतिलोमेन दुर्जनम् ।
> आत्मतुल्यबलं शत्रुं विनयेन बलेन वा ॥ ०७-१० ॥

One should deal with a powerful man by conciliation and with a wicked man by
opposition. An enemy of equal strength should be dealt with either through
diplomacy or by force.


07-11

> बाहुवीर्यं बलं राज्ञां ब्रह्मणो ब्रह्मविद्बली ।
> रूपयौवनमाधुर्यं स्त्रीणां बलमनुत्तमम् ॥ ०७-११ ॥

The strength of kings lies in the might of their arms; a Brahmin who knows
Brahman has Brahman as his strength. Incomparable is the strength of women,
which is beauty, youth, and sweetness.


07-12

> नात्यन्तं सरलैर्भाव्यं गत्वा पश्य वनस्थलीम् ।
> छिद्यन्ते सरलास्तत्र कुब्जास्तिष्ठन्ति पादपाः ॥ ०७-१२ ॥

One should never be excessively straight; go and observe the forest. There,
the straight trees are cut down, while the crooked ones stand.


07-13

> यत्रोदकं तत्र वसन्ति हंसा-
> स्तथैव शुष्कं परिवर्जयन्ति ।
> न हंसतुल्येन नरेण भाव्यं
> पुनस्त्यजन्तः पुनराश्रयन्ते ॥ ०७-१३ ॥

When there is water, swans dwell there, and when it dries up, they abandon
it. A man should not be like a swan, ever abandoning and then seeking refuge
again.


07-14

> उपार्जितानां वित्तानां त्याग एव हि रक्षणम् ।
> तडागोदरसंस्थानां परीवाह इवाम्भसाम् ॥ ०७-१४ ॥

The protection of acquired wealth lies indeed in its being given away, just
like the protection of water lying in the belly of a tank lies in its outflow.


07-15

> यस्यार्थास्तस्य मित्राणि यस्यार्थास्तस्य बान्धवाः ।
> यस्यार्थाः स पुमाँल्लोके यस्यार्थाः स च पण्डितः ॥ ०७-१५ ॥

He who has wealth has friends; he who has wealth has kinsmen; he who has
wealth is a man in the world; he who has wealth is a wise man.


07-16

> स्वर्गस्थितानामिह जीवलोके
> चत्वारि चिह्नानि वसन्ति देहे ।
> दानप्रसङ्गो मधुरा च वाणी
> देवार्चनं ब्राह्मणतर्पणं च ॥ ०७-१६ ॥

In the body of those destined for heaven, four signs dwell while yet in this
world of the living: a natural inclination for charity, sweet speech, worship
of the gods, and the constant satisfaction of Brahmins.


07-17

> अत्यन्तकोपः कटुका च वाणी
> दरिद्रता च स्वजनेषु वैरम् ।
> नीचप्रसङ्गः कुलहीनसेवा
> चिह्नानि देहे नरकस्थितानाम् ॥ ०७-१७ ॥

Excessive anger, harsh speech, poverty, enmity towards one's own people,
association with the low, and serving those of low family — these are the
signs in the body of those destined for hell.


07-18

> गम्यते यदि मृगेन्द्रमन्दिरं
> लभ्यते करिकपालमौक्तिकम् ।
> जम्बुकालयगते च प्राप्यते
> वत्सपुच्छखरचर्मखण्डनम् ॥ ०७-१८ ॥

If one goes to the abode of the lord of beasts, one finds elephant tusks and
pearls. But if one goes to the den of a jackal, one obtains only pieces of
calf-hide and donkey-skin.


07-19

> शुनः पुच्छमिव व्यर्थं जीवितं विद्यया विना ।
> न गुह्यगोपने शक्तं न च दंशनिवारणे ॥ ०७-१९ ॥

A life without learning is as useless as a dog's tail, for it can neither hide
the private parts nor ward off flies.


07-20

> वाचां शौचं च मनसः शौचमिन्द्रियनिग्रहः ।
> सर्वभूतदयाशौचमेतच्छौचं परार्थिनाम् ॥ ०७-२० ॥

Purity of speech, purity of mind, restraint of the senses, and compassion
towards all beings — this is the purity prescribed for those who seek the
highest good.


07-21

> पुष्पे गन्धं तिले तैलं काष्ठेऽग्निं पयसि घृतम् ।
> इक्षौ गुडं तथा देहे पश्यात्मानं विवेकतः ॥ ०७-२१ ॥

See the fragrance in the flower, the oil in the sesame seed, the fire in the
wood, the ghee in the milk, the jaggery in the sugarcane; likewise, through
discernment, behold the Self within the body.

Chanakya Niti

Chapter 2

                               Adhyāya 2

02-01

> अनृतं साहसं माया मूर्खत्वमतिलोभिता ।
> अशौचत्वं निर्दयत्वं स्त्रीणां दोषाः स्वभावजाः ॥ ०२-०१ ॥

Falsehood, rashness, deception, foolishness, excessive greed, impurity, and
cruelty — these are the natural faults of women.


02-02

> भोज्यं भोजनशक्तिश्च रतिशक्तिर्वराङ्गना ।
> विभवो दानशक्तिश्च नाल्पस्य तपसः फलम् ॥ ०२-०२ ॥

The ability to enjoy food, the capacity for pleasure along with a beautiful
woman, prosperity combined with the power of giving — these are the rare
fruits of no small austerity.


02-03

> यस्य पुत्रो वशीभूतो भार्या छन्दानुगामिनी ।
> विभवे यश्च सन्तुष्टस्तस्य स्वर्ग इहैव हि ॥ ०२-०३ ॥

He whose son is under his control, whose wife follows his will, and who is
content with his wealth — for him, heaven is verily here on earth.


02-04

> ते पुत्रा ये पितुर्भक्ताः स पिता यस्तु पोषकः ।
> तन्मित्रं यत्र विश्वासः सा भार्या यत्र निर्वृतिः ॥ ०२-०४ ॥

They alone are true sons who are devoted to their father; he alone is a true
father who sustains his children; that alone is a true friend in whom trust is
placed; and she alone is a true wife in whom one finds peace.


02-05

> परोक्षे कार्यहन्तारं प्रत्यक्षे प्रियवादिनम् ।
> वर्जयेत्तादृशं मित्रं विषकुम्भं पयोमुखम् ॥ ०२-०५ ॥

One should avoid a friend who, behind one's back, destroys one's work and who,
to one's face, speaks sweetly — such a friend is like a pot of poison with a
surface of milk.


02-06

> न विश्वसेत्कुमित्रे च मित्रे चापि न विश्वसेत् ।
> कदाचित्कुपितं मित्रं सर्वं गुह्यं प्रकाशयेत् ॥ ०२-०६ ॥

Do not trust a false friend, nor place excessive trust even in a true friend;
for a friend, when provoked at some point, may reveal all one's secrets.


02-07

> मनसा चिन्तितं कार्यं वाचा नैव प्रकाशयेत् ।
> मन्त्रेण रक्षयेद्गूढं कार्ये चापि नियोजयेत् ॥ ०२-०७ ॥

A matter pondered in the mind should never be revealed by speech; one should
protect it by secrecy and put it into action only with counsel.


02-08

> कष्टं च खलु मूर्खत्वं कष्टं च खलु यौवनम् ।
> कष्टात्कष्टतरं चैव परगेहनिवासनम् ॥ ०२-०८ ॥

Wretched indeed is foolishness; wretched indeed is youth; but more wretched
than the most wretched is dwelling in another's house.


02-09

> शैले शैले च माणिक्यं मौक्तिकं न गजे गजे ।
> साधवो न हि सर्वत्र चन्दनं न वने वने ॥ ०२-०९ ॥

Not every mountain holds a gem, not every elephant bears a pearl; the good are
not found everywhere, and sandalwood does not grow in every forest.


02-10

> पुत्राश्च विविधैः शीलैर्नियोज्याः सततं बुधैः ।
> नीतिज्ञाः शीलसम्पन्ना भवन्ति कुलपूजिताः ॥ ०२-१० ॥

Sons should constantly be engaged by the wise in various disciplines of
character; those who are versed in ethics and endowed with good character
become the honour of their family.


02-11

> माता शत्रुः पिता वैरी याभ्यां बाला न पाठिताः ।
> सभामध्ये न शोभन्ते हंसमध्ये बको यथा ॥ ०२-११ ॥

A mother who does not educate her children is an enemy, and so is a father;
just as a crane does not shine in the midst of swans, so untaught children do
not shine in an assembly.


02-12

> लालनाद्बहवो दोषास्ताडने बहवो गुणाः ।
> तस्मात्पुत्रं च शिष्यं च ताडयेन्न तु लालयेत् ॥ ०२-१२ ॥

Many are the faults that arise from indulgence; many are the virtues produced
by chastisement. Therefore, one should chastise both son and pupil, and never
indulge them.


02-13

> श्लोकेन वा तदर्धेन तदर्धार्धाक्षरेण वा ।
> अबन्ध्यं दिवसं कुर्याद्दानाध्ययनकर्मभिः ॥ ०२-१३ ॥

Let no day pass in vain; let it be filled, even if only with a single verse,
half a verse, or even half a syllable, with acts of giving and study.


02-14

> कान्तावियोगः स्वजनापमानं
> ऋणस्य शेषं कुनृपस्य सेवा ।
> दारिद्र्यभावाद्विमुखं च मित्रं
> विनाग्निना पञ्च दहन्ति कायम् ॥ ०२-१४ ॥

Separation from the beloved, disgrace from one's own people, a lingering debt,
service to a bad king, poverty, and a friend grown indifferent — these five
burn the body even without fire.


02-15

> नदीतीरे च ये वृक्षाः परगेहेषु कामिनी ।
> मन्त्रहीनाश्च राजानः शीघ्रं नश्यन्त्यसंशयम् ॥ ०२-१५ ॥

Trees growing on a riverbank, a mistress in another's house, and kings without
sound counsel perish swiftly, without a doubt.


02-16

> बलं विद्या च विप्राणां राज्ञां सैन्यं बलं तथा ।
> बलं वित्तं च वैश्यानां शूद्राणां पारिचर्यकम् ॥ ०२-१६ ॥

Learning is the strength of Brahmins, the army is the strength of kings,
wealth is the strength of Vaiśyas, and service is the strength of Śūdras.


02-17

> निर्धनं पुरुषं वेश्या प्रजा भग्नं नृपं त्यजेत् ।
> खगा वीतफलं वृक्षं भुक्त्वा चाभ्यागतो गृहम् ॥ ०२-१७ ॥

A courtesan abandons a penniless man, subjects abandon a defeated king, birds
abandon a tree that bears no fruit, and a guest, having eaten, abandons the
house.


02-18

> गृहीत्वा दक्षिणां विप्रास्त्यजन्ति यजमानकम् ।
> प्राप्तविद्या गुरुं शिष्या दग्धारण्यं मृगास्तथा ॥ ०२-१८ ॥

Priests abandon the sacrificer after taking their dakṣiṇā; disciples abandon
their teacher after obtaining their learning; and animals abandon a forest
that has been consumed by fire.


02-19

> दुराचारी दुरादृष्टिर्दुरावासी च दुर्जनः ।
> यन्मैत्री क्रियते पुंभिर्नरः शीघ्रं विनश्यति ॥ ०२-१९ ॥

A man who is ill-behaved, of evil aspect, of wicked dwelling, and malicious —
the friendship that is formed with him by men quickly leads a man to
destruction.


02-20

> समाने शोभते प्रीतिः राज्ञि सेवा च शोभते ।
> वाणिज्यं व्यवहारेषु दिव्या स्त्री शोभते गृहे ॥ ०२-२० ॥

Affection shines best among equals; service shines best when rendered to a
king; commerce shines in business dealings; and a beautiful woman shines best
at home.
